A country-specific early-warning approach for geopolitical and economic-policy uncertainty, built on GDELT event data and logistic regression, for Germany and the USA. These three pages hold the figures behind the defence: what the submitted thesis measured, what a rebuild against an external target measured afterwards, and what runs daily today.

Both sets of figures come from the same pipeline. The difference is where the target variable comes from: inside the GDELT event series for the thesis, outside it — market volatility, policy uncertainty, geopolitical risk — for the rebuild. A coin flip scores 0.500.

The research question

To what extent can phases of heightened geopolitical and economic-policy uncertainty be detected early, country-specifically, from news-based event data in GDELT using logistic regression — and which event categories contribute most to the warning signal? The question as submitted

How to read
these pages

ROC-AUC — probability that a randomly chosen warning day is ranked above a randomly chosen calm day0.5 = chance, 1.0 = perfect
Brier score — mean squared error of the probability itselflower is better
Bracketed pairs — 95 % bootstrap intervalspans zero = nothing shown
Standardised coefficientscomparable inside one model, not between two
