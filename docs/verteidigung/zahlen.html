0.330 is below chance, not merely weak. With 27 times more American than German events, a pooled model learns which country a row belongs to and applies that as if it were the risk. A country indicator buys a different intercept, not a different relationship — only separate models fix it. This comparison is why the thesis is country-specific.

The warning threshold

Germany · threshold / warning days / episodes / recall0.20 · 150 · 24 · 1.00
USA · threshold / warning days / episodes / recall0.60 · 43 · 10 · 0.75
Both thresholds come from the same rule — highest F1 on out-of-fold predictions — and the rule produced two different answers. Where the line sits is a decision about the cost of a false alarm, not a statistic.

The sign of tone_avg is a trap. The coefficient is negative and the feature itself takes negative values, so a more negative tone raises the modelled probability. Negativity increases the warning; it does not reduce it. And the answer to the second half of the research question is the first row: the strongest single predictor is not violence but the sheer density of reporting.
