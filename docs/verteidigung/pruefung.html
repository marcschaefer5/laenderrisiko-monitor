The check

The thesis named two gaps in its own limitations chapter: the target was derived from the same event series as the features, and no model was ever compared against a benchmark. This page is what happens when both gaps are closed.

One finding survives both placebos, and no more than that. On the 20 documented events the warning does rise measurably. It does not survive a shift of the threshold or a split of the period — so it is evidence that the signal exists, not that it can be relied on operationally. Keeping those two apart is the whole point of the rebuild.

Counted news events do not anticipate uncertainty that is measured somewhere else. What they do describe is the present: how much is being reported, how negatively, and how that compares to a country's own recent normal. That is what the live monitor was built to show, and why every figure there stands next to its benchmark.
